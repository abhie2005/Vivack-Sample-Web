How it works

From ambiguity to clarity.

01

Listen & Unpack

Before a single mark is made, I sit with the problem. Client conversations, competitive context, user behaviour — understanding the brief behind the brief.

02

Explore Wide

Divergent sketching across 3–5 distinct directions. The goal is to surprise myself and find the edge the obvious solution would have missed.

03

Converge with Precision

One direction, refined obsessively. Every decision is justified — nothing exists for decoration alone.

04

Deliver & Document

Final assets with a usage guide your whole team can actually use. Clean handoff, no loose ends.

About

I think in systems, but I design for moments.

My name is Aryan Mehta. I studied Industrial Design at NID Ahmedabad and taught myself graphic design by obsessively pulling apart everything I found beautiful. Six years later, those two languages live in the same sentence for me.

I've worked with early-stage startups, established consumer brands, and cultural institutions — always with the same intent: make something that doesn't need explaining.

When I'm not in the studio you'll find me at a material library, a second-hand bookshop, or deep in the footnotes of a type history essay.
